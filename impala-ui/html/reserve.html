Replenishment

Sells accumulated float back into the asset the pool pays out. Caps of 0 mean unconfigured — a cycle refuses to run until they are set. The fiat leg books as in transit until someone confirms the bank credit, because the bridge cannot see one.

Refunds

Deposits the reserve cannot use, returned to their payer. Frozen rows had an ambiguous submit — the payment may have landed, so nothing is retried automatically.

Unmatched Deposits

Inflows to the reserve account that no order could accept (late, underpaid, wrong asset, unknown memo). Known assets are already credited to inventory; disposition is per runbook.
