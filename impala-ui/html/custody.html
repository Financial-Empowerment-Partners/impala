Custody

The custodial payment brake and spend caps for POST /managed-account/sign, every payment intent the bridge has claimed, and the reconciliation snapshots that attest the reserve against the chain. Caps of 0 mean unconfigured — signing refuses until both are set.

Policy

Anyone with the treasurer or admin role may pull the brake; only an admin releases it, with the confirmation phrase the bridge serves, and never while an intent’s on-chain fate is unknown unless forced.

Payment Intents

Every custodial payment is claimed as a write-ahead intent before the seed is opened, and its signed hash is persisted before submit. Ambiguous rows had an unknown submit outcome: they are resolved by hash (by the sweep after 600s, or here after reading the chain) and never resubmitted.

Custodial Accounts

Reconciliation Snapshots

One daily snapshot per UTC date is recorded automatically. A row is attested only when Horizon was fresh, every custodial address was read, and every invariant held — a lagging day is kept as evidence, not as a pass.
