Stellar key (SEP-10) (optional: all three or none)

For a card that holds a Stellar key (applet 0.3 and later), read from the card’s GET_STELLAR_KEY response after personalization; the Android demo’s Card Lab shows them. The bridge verifies both cross-signatures against this account id, card id and EC public key. With these, the card can sign in through SEP-10.

Never fund a card’s Stellar address. The card signs only SEP-10 challenges, so nothing can sign a transaction that spends from it: anything sent there is lost.

The bridge registers a card only under the account you are signed in as.
